Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

This is the personal portfolio of Lennon (@lenodrips), at lenodrips-create.github.io/lennon-sells. It is a static website: it has no accounts, no database, no analytics and no advertising. This policy explains the little information that is involved when you visit, and who handles it.

Who is responsible

Lennon runs this site. For anything about privacy, email lenodrips@gmail.com.

What this site collects

  • Nothing is stored by the site itself. It sets no cookies and uses no local storage, analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or advertising tools.
  • The email bar does not collect your address. When you type your email and press the arrow, the site checks the address looks valid in your browser and then opens your own email app with a message to Lennon. Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose to send that email yourself.
  • Emails you send. If you email Lennon, Lennon receives your email address, name (if your email shows one) and whatever you write, and uses it only to read and reply to you.

Service providers that receive technical data

Like every website, loading these pages sends standard technical information (such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested) to the services that deliver them:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the site. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • Amazon CloudFront delivers the two background films (the opening film and the film behind the email bar). These files are served from a CloudFront account that is not run by Lennon, so the operator of that account and Amazon may receive the same technical data. See the AWS Privacy Notice.
  • Gmail (Google) handles email sent to Lennon. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Fonts, images and scripts are served from this site itself, so they do not involve any other company.

How long information is kept

  • The site keeps no visitor data, so there is nothing for it to delete.
  • Emails are kept as long as the conversation is useful and are deleted within 24 months of the last message, unless you ask for earlier deletion or they need to be kept for a legal reason.
  • Server logs kept by GitHub or Amazon follow their own retention periods, described in the policies linked above.

Sharing

Lennon does not sell, rent or share your information, and does not use it for advertising. It would only be disclosed if required by law.

Your choices and rights

You can ask Lennon what information Lennon holds about you (in practice, only emails you have sent), ask for a copy, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted, by emailing lenodrips@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR may give you these and other rights, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has emailed personal information, contact Lennon and it will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
