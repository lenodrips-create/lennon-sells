About me

@lenodrips

I'm Lennon. I build what I imagine, from Greencastle, Pennsylvania.

Greencastle is a small town in Franklin County, in the Cumberland Valley of south-central Pennsylvania, a few miles above the Maryland line. It has a town square with a fountain at its centre, brick storefronts on every side, farmland out past the edge of town and Interstate 81 running along it. It's a quiet, friendly place where people know each other, and that's where all of this gets made.

A small town is a good place to build things. There's room to think, no hype to chase and nobody telling you a project is too big. If I want something that doesn't exist yet, I sit down and make it. That's how I ended up writing my own web browser instead of just using one.

I'm a developer who likes building the whole thing: the engine, the interface and the page that tells people about it. Most of my work starts as a question. Could I make my own web browser? That became Kepler, written in C++ on Qt and Chromium. Could a full code studio run inside a browser tab? That became CTerm Studio, with a C core compiled to WebAssembly.

I care about how software feels as much as what it does: fast, private, and a little bit cinematic. Everything I build here in Greencastle goes out to anyone, anywhere, so I want it to be as good as anything made in a big city.
