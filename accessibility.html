Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed 8 October 2026

Lennon wants this site to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, zoom, or reduced-motion settings. The target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

What has been done

  • Everything can be reached by keyboard. A "Skip to content" link is the first thing you can tab to, and focus is clearly outlined.
  • Each project photo in the 3D sphere and in the grid can be focused and opened with Enter or Space. The photo viewer is announced as a dialog, moves focus to its Close button, closes with Escape, and returns focus to the photo you opened.
  • The menu moves focus into its links when opened and back to the menu button when closed.
  • Pages use a logical heading order, images have text alternatives, and the email field has a label and reports errors in text.
  • Text colours were adjusted so that body text and small labels meet at least a 4.5:1 contrast ratio against the background.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, the site shortens or removes its animations and transitions.
  • The background films are decorative and have no sound. The opening film can be skipped with the Skip button.
  • Text is sized in a way that still works when the browser is zoomed to 200%.

Known limitations

  • Dragging to rotate the 3D photo sphere needs a pointer. Keyboard users can tab through the same photos, use the grid view, or use the Projects list further down the page, which contains the same projects as plain links.
  • The night-sky background and the sphere move as you scroll. Turning on your device's reduced-motion setting tones this down but does not remove all movement.

Report a barrier

If something on this site is hard to use or you need content in another format, email lenodrips@gmail.com with the page and what went wrong. Lennon aims to reply within 5 working days.
